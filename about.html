About Us

The people behind Tech ConnectIT

Tech ConnectIT.AI is an AI-first company founded on more than 30 years of enterprise infrastructure. We build AI products and advise on AI adoption — with humans firmly in control of the systems we deploy.

Frank Koziol

Founder & President, Tech ConnectIT.AI

Frank Koziol is a technology executive with three decades of leadership across network engineering, cybersecurity, and large-scale IT operations. At Boeing, he led infrastructure and analytics organizations and drove 128 safety and quality systems to Green — consolidating them to 21 and saving $1M a year. Earlier, he founded and ran Netigy, a network services company, and directed network and security architecture at Fujitsu.

His AI experience spans enterprise operations and product design. At Boeing, he architected Bella Blue, a predictive analytics product deployed across Safety and Quality. His Boeing AI portfolio also included Connected Aircraft — an AI-enabled predictive analytics product that uses in-flight data to drive down operator costs through fuel savings and maintenance optimization. As founder of Tech ConnectIT.AI, he designs AI governance and network-identity architectures, and is building The Voice Between Us — a voice-based connection platform powered by the Empathy Cortex, a proprietary model that reads voice cues to match people.

He holds a Master's in Computer Information Science from La Salle University. His current work centers on AI governance, network identity, and designing AI systems that stay under human control.
