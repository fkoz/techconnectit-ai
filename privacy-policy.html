Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Who we are

Tech ConnectIT, LLC is an AI-first company: an AI consulting practice, and the maker of The Voice Between Us. This policy covers the whole company — our websites (techconnectit.ai and thevoicebetweenus.com), the emails you send us, and the information shared with us in the course of our work.

The Voice Between Us has its own privacy policy covering the pilot tester list and the product; where the two differ for the product, the product policy governs the product. This page is the company's general policy, in plain language.

What we collect

Only what a working business needs:

  • What you send us. If you email us — at frank@techconnectit.ai or hello@techconnectit.ai — we receive your name, your email address, and whatever you write. There are no forms harvesting data on our websites; the contact route is email, and it goes to a person.
  • Business information. If you engage us, or explore engaging us, we hold the contact details and project information you share so we can do the work: names, work emails, and the documents and details you give us about your project.
  • Tester list emails. If you join The Voice Between Us pilot tester list, we hold your email address under the product's privacy policy.
  • Standard web hosting logs. Our websites are hosted on GitHub Pages, which automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. We don't use this to identify you or to build a profile of you.

Our websites use no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising trackers.

What we use it for

  • To answer your inquiry.
  • To deliver the work we've been engaged to do.
  • To run the business: scheduling, billing, and records we're required to keep.

Nothing else. Your information is never used to market someone else's products, and client information is never used for any purpose outside that client's work.

What we never do

  • We never sell personal information.
  • We never share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never use client information to train public AI models.
  • We never add you to a mailing list you didn't join. (The only list we keep is the Voice Between Us tester list, and joining it is your choice.)

Sharing, when the work requires it

We share information only as far as the work requires: with the cloud and software providers that host or process it on our behalf (for example, our email runs on Microsoft 365 and our websites on GitHub Pages), under their confidentiality obligations, and only where a disclosure is required by law. If an engagement requires other sharing, that's agreed with the client in writing first.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiry emails: until the conversation is resolved, plus a reasonable period in case you write again.
  • Client and business records: for as long as the relationship and our legal and tax obligations require.
  • Tester list emails: as described in the product policy — until launch communications end or you ask to be removed.

When information is no longer needed, it's deleted.

Your choices

Your information is yours. Email us at hello@techconnectit.ai to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted — where the law allows deletion, we'll delete it and confirm. Business records we're legally required to keep (invoices, tax records) are the only exception, and we'll tell you if that applies.

Security

We protect business information with the ordinary care of a security practice: company email and files sit in access-controlled accounts, and client material is handled as confidential by default.

Changes to this policy

As the company grows, this policy will be updated. When we make changes, we'll post the updated policy here and update the effective date above.

Talk to us

Questions about this policy, or about your information? Write to hello@techconnectit.ai. A real person reads that inbox.
