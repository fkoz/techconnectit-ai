How we work

Two disciplines govern every engagement: how the work is sequenced, and what the evidence says actually drives the result.

Delivery
Value-Driven Delivery

We sequence and deliver work by business value — not by hours billed, and not by ticket order. Before anything is built, the outcomes are named and the baseline is agreed in writing, so both sides know what "worth it" means before the work starts. The highest-value work goes first, and progress is measured in value delivered: cost removed, cycle time cut, risk retired. It is the delivery discipline behind our guarantee — proof, or you don't pay.

  • Value first — the work that returns the most goes first; everything else waits its turn.
  • Measured, not reported — progress is shown against the baseline agreed up front, in numbers you can check.
  • No value, no invoice — if the value isn't proven, the assessment costs you nothing. That's the guarantee, applied to delivery.
How value gets measured
  • ROI — Return on investment: the benefits received against the money invested, expressed as a percentage.
  • IRR — Internal rate of return: the rate that makes tomorrow's money worth a defined amount in today's money — how we compare returns across time.
  • NPV — Net present value: the total present value of expected future inflows minus the initial investment — the test of whether a project is financially viable at all.

Evidence
People Over Processes

The estimating math has said it for decades. COCOMO — the Constructive Cost Model, long used to estimate what drives the cost of a project — weighs the factors that move a project's outcome, and tools and processes are not the lever.

AI Security

An audit of where AI leaves your company vulnerable — including the shadow AI already inside it — plus the watch tooling to keep it covered, implemented for you.
